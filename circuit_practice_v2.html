About the circuit pictures. The moving dots show the current, flowing from + round to −. Faster dots mean more current, so you can compare the paths within one circuit, but the speed is not the real speed of the charges (and it is scaled down when a circuit's currents are large). The real-parts view is a drawing: the resistor colour bands match the given values, but some values, such as 6 kΩ, are chosen for easy arithmetic and are not standard parts.
