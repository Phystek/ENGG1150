MISSION

Deliver the power. Across the unit, students design and build circuits that deliver required power to a load, from a resistor and an LED, to a full RC transient, to an AC signal restored through a phase-correction circuit they've designed themselves.

WEEK BY WEEK

Lectures, tutorials & labs

SGTA tutorials and practical labs both begin in Week 2. The totals are fixed by the CMS: 12 SGTA sessions (24 hours) and 6 practical labs (12 hours). The Arc 1 lab worksheets (Labs 1 to 3) are drafted at v0.1 and run on a fortnightly cycle, so each is shown as Week 2 or 3, Week 4 or 5, and Week 6 or 7. Arc 2 placements and all SGTA questions remain proposed examples, still to be worked out with Steve. Every lab is assessed through the Lab Portfolio. The two labs flagged DEMONSTRATION (Torch and Black Box) end in a live demonstration that forms a key part of that portfolio.

Lectures run as lectorials: content is broken into short segments with frequent full-class problem-solving activities throughout. The gold Maths line under each week flags one suggested point where Steve leads a dedicated maths segment; it is a starting example, not the only maths content in that lecture.

THE LAB PROGRAM IN DETAIL

Two arcs, six labs, one skill set each

The two arcs and their order are confirmed. Arc 1 (DC circuits, Labs 1 to 3) has draft worksheets: click a lab to see its time plan, portfolio items and marking split. Arc 2 (Labs 4 to 6) is still an outline. Instrumentation is introduced progressively: the multimeter and bench power supply in Lab 1, then the function generator and oscilloscope in Lab 4, so each arc only asks students to learn one new instrument family before it is needed. Every lab is assessed through the Lab Portfolio, and the Torch (Lab 3) and Black Box (Lab 6) labs each end in a live demonstration that forms a key part of it.

The bars show how each 2-hour lab is split across its parts (A, B, C and so on). A gold underline marks a part that produces a portfolio item. Week numbers for Arc 2 are proposed placements.
