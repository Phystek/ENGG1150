MISSION

Deliver the power, shape the signal. Arc 1 is about delivering the right amount of power to a load, safely and efficiently, ending in a battery-powered LED torch designed to a specification (Practical Demonstration 1). Arc 2 is about signals: how capacitors, time constants and phase change a signal's shape, ending with students working out what a hidden circuit does to a signal and designing a circuit to correct it (Practical Demonstration 2). The written exam tests the analysis behind both halves.

WEEK BY WEEK

Lectures, tutorials & labs

SGTA tutorials and practical labs both begin in Week 2. The totals are fixed by the CMS: 12 SGTA sessions (24 hours) and 6 practical labs (12 hours). The Arc 1 lab worksheets (Labs 1 to 3) are drafted at v0.1, need reworking for the new assessment structure, and run on a fortnightly cycle, so each is shown as Week 2 or 3, Week 4 or 5, and Week 6 or 7. Arc 2 placements and all SGTA questions remain proposed examples, still to be worked out with Steve. Labs 1, 2, 4 and 5 are not assessed: they build the skills for the two labs flagged ASSESSED, which end each arc with a practical demonstration (Lab 3, 30%; Lab 6, 20%).

Lectures run as lectorials: content is broken into short segments with frequent full-class problem-solving activities throughout. The gold Maths line under each week flags one suggested point where Steve leads a dedicated maths segment; it is a starting example, not the only maths content in that lecture.

THE LAB PROGRAM IN DETAIL

Two arcs, six labs, one skill set each

The two arcs and their order are confirmed. Arc 1 (DC circuits, Labs 1 to 3) has draft worksheets, but they were written for the earlier Lab Portfolio model and are flagged NEEDS REWORK until they are revised for the new assessment structure. Click a lab to see its time plan, worksheet items and what needs changing. Arc 2 (Labs 4 to 6) is still an outline. Instrumentation is introduced progressively: the multimeter and bench power supply in Lab 1, then the function generator and oscilloscope in Lab 4, so each arc only asks students to learn one new instrument family before it is needed. Only the arc-final labs are assessed: the Torch (Lab 3) is Practical Demonstration 1 and the Black Box (Lab 6) is Practical Demonstration 2.
